# The Dancer — Production Design

> **Status:** active boss / retained canon
> **Animation pipeline:** deliberately open

The Dancer represents **performance and choreography**.

## Identity

- broken music-box ballerina / automaton
- graceful, expressive, uncanny and increasingly unstable
- midnight blue, cold porcelain and electric violet palette
- restrained antique-gold / bronze mechanical joints and trim
- established porcelain and mechanical anatomy
- ribbons, ornamentation and celestial motifs
- rough, grungy semi-pixel rendering

## Movement grammar

Use:

- ballet phrases
- elongated arcs
- mechanical repetitions
- sudden freezes
- overextended joints
- ribbon / skirt follow-through
- increasing corruption of otherwise controlled choreography

## Encounter language

- **Spiral Cascade** remains a recurring signature attack.
- **Broken Coda** remains an active major-special proposal.

## Damage arc

Damage and low health progressively corrupt controlled choreography rather than
turning her into a generic shaking or flashing enemy.

## Animation pipeline

Not locked.

Older layered-rig instructions are historical proposals, not current canon.

During production evaluate:

- whole-character frame-by-frame 2D
- carefully layered 2D
- hybrid approaches

The decision must serve the Dancer's silhouette, acting and animation quality,
rather than forcing her into the Conductor's solution.
