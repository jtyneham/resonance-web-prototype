# Broken Resonance Mote — Player Design

> **Status:** selected player identity / production details open

The player is the **Broken Resonance Mote**.

## Identity

Preserve:

- compact asymmetrical graphite shell / cracked tuning chamber
- warm luminous fissure or core
- two short mismatched upper prongs that subtly evoke a damaged tuning fork
- a small number of detached fragments
- worn blackened material with restrained aged-ivory edging
- no face or eyes
- no humanoid anatomy
- no conventional music-note silhouette

## Gameplay readability

The player must remain readable at portrait-phone scale.

Core states eventually require clear acting for:

- grounded idle
- lateral move
- jump
- landing
- absorption
- charged
- counterattack
- damage
- defeat / recovery where applicable

Each boss encounter may shape the returned-resonance projectile differently while
the player control and charge logic remain consistent.
